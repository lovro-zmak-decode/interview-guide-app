## Context

What situation forced a decision. The constraints that were actually in play. Written so a
reader two years from now understands why the obvious alternative was not free.

## Decision

What we decided, in one or two sentences, in the active voice: "We will ...". Follow with the
mechanics only if they are not obvious.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and the one-line reason each lost. Delete this section if there was
no real alternative.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we are now committed to. Include the known
costs; a consequences section with no downsides was not thought through.
